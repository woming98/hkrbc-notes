## Key Topics Covered

- **Economic Balance Sheet**: Current Estimate, TVOG, MOCE, Reinsurance Recoverables
- **Matching Adjustment**: Asset Spread, Asset Duration, Constant Prescribed Spread Component, Dynamic MA
- **Prescribed Capital Amount (PCA)**: Interest Rate, Credit Spread, Equity, Insurance, Operational Risk modules; LAC of FDB & Deferred Tax
- **ALM Modelling**: ESG (Hull-White 1F, GBM, Heston, CIR), yield curve bootstrapping, Smith-Wilson, HKD LLP & UFR
- **TVOG**: Stochastic vs deterministic valuation, variance reduction, Dynamic Policyholder Behaviour
- **Participating Fund TVOG**: Asset Share model, GL34 compliance, bonus smoothing, stochastic-on-stochastic
- **Prophet Workflow**: End-to-end production workflow for Par fund TVOG
